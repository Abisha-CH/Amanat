Non-Custodial Architecture

Amanat never touches or holds your funds.

Traditional fundraising platforms lock contributions inside third-party bank accounts. Amanat replaces black-box custodianship with open civic ledgering: every donor sends funds directly via banking rails, while every receipt, voucher, and disbursement requires multi-signatory verification.
