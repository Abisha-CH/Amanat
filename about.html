About Amanat

Restoring Civic Trust Through Radical Financial Sunlight.

A non-custodial, open-ledger framework engineered for university societies, student clubs, and grassroots community groups.

The Problem

Traditional donation boxes, loose cash envelopes, and informal WhatsApp groups suffer from opaque record-keeping. Receipts are routinely misplaced, expense updates remain fragmented, and members are burdened by recurring donor skepticism and unverified accusations.

  • ✕ Unverifiable cash collections & private personal accounts
  • ✕ Undocumented last-minute vendor cash disbursements
  • ✕ Erosion of peer confidence and student community engagement

The Solution

Amanat provides an open verification loop where every single rupee pledged is backed by an external bank transaction reference (IBFT/Raast), and every rupee spent is corroborated by an itemized, publicly accessible vendor invoice and treasurer seal.

  • ✓ Direct external bank verification with dual reconciliations
  • ✓ Granular public ledgers accessible to everyone without paywalls
  • ✓ Permanent digital paper-trail safeguarding student leadership integrity
Non-Custodial Guarantee

Amanat Never Touches or Pools Your Money.

Direct Raast, IBFT, or Cash to verified student treasurers: All financial transactions occur strictly outside the platform on official banking rails. Amanat serves solely as the immutable verification, auditing, and receipt-matching ledger.

Zero transaction fees or platform deductions
No third-party wallet or balance lock-in

Framework Roles

The 4-Role Verification Ecosystem

Engineered separation of responsibilities guarantees cross-checks across all campaign phases.

Organizer

Launches verified campaign objectives, outlines phased milestones, and posts official vendor reimbursement requests.

Initiator & Creator

Treasurer

Cross-references banking transactions against member reference IDs, verifies authenticity, and uploads stamped invoices.

Fiduciary Custodian

Contributor

Executes direct external transfers, logs transaction IDs into the platform, and tracks real-time utilization.

Direct Backer

Public Visitor

Inspects unedited expense bills, balance sheets, and audit trails without requiring account registration.

Audited Lifecycle

Step-by-Step Approval Protocol

Every movement on the ledger is deterministic, verifiable, and permanent.

01

Verified Launch

Organizer publishes campaign mission, target amount, and line-item budget milestones for public inspection.

02

External Pledge

Donor sends funds directly to student treasurer via Raast/IBFT and enters the bank reference code into Amanat.

03

Dual Reconciliation

Treasurer verifies incoming credits on official bank statements and signs off on the matching transaction token.

04

Public Archival

Invoices & vendor payment vouchers are uploaded and locked permanently into the public transparency ledger.
