About This Campaign

Together, we can provide better educational facilities for underprivileged children in our community. In Sector H-9 and adjoining suburban clusters, public primary schools currently lack adequate dual-desks and modern interactive chalkboards.

This initiative was established by student volunteers in coordination with the local Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) to renovate three degraded elementary classrooms, construct sturdy bench sets, and provide whole-year textbook parcels.

Accountability Guarantee

  • Zero administrative cuts or platform fees taken by AMANAT.
  • Dual signature authorization: All receipts require verification from both student committee lead and local PTA treasurer.
  • Direct vendor settlement receipts published openly with unredacted invoice scans.
