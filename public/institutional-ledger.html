How to independently verify a fund

No account, no fee, and no intermediary. Verification is designed to be repeated by any donor or community auditor.

1

Open the sealed audit certificate

Each fund row exposes its SHA-256 digest. Compare the digest against the treasurer-published certificate before reviewing individual vouchers.

2

Match receipts to bank statements

Every outflow links to an itemized receipt and a dual-treasurer signature. Inflows must match the published bank credit line.

3

Report discrepancies publicly

Any mismatch can be raised through the Oversight Board channel. Disputed entries remain visible in the journal until resolved and re-sealed.
